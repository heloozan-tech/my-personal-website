03 — Process

No two products are alike.
The way I work stays consistent.

Four steps, every project. Research first, so every pixel answers a real question.

Step 01 / 04

Discover

I start by understanding the people, the business and the problem. Interviews, audits and research synthesis make sure we solve the right thing before a single pixel is pushed.

User interviewsStakeholder workshopCompetitive auditAffinity mapping
Step 02 / 04

Define

Insights become a clear direction: personas, journeys and a sharp problem statement the whole team can rally behind.

PersonasJourney mappingProblem statementInformation architecture

I explore ideas fast through wireframes and interactive prototypes, then test them with real users. Every round of feedback sharpens the solution.
